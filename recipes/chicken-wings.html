Crispy Baked Chicken Wings

Description

These crispy oven Baked Chicken Wings are so easy to prepare and you don't even need to bother with all the grease from frying

Whether you're watching football, basketball or even your favourite show, these wings are baked to crispy perfection, and then tossed in the most delicious sweet and spicy buffalo sauce. They're totally addicting and perfect for game day! Make them ASAP, and then thank me later!

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil (optional)

Buffalo Sauce:

  • 120 ml hot sauce
  • 60 g unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan).
  2. Pat the chicken wings dry using kitchen paper.
  3. Place the wings in a large bowl.
  4. Add the baking powder, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
  5. Toss the wings until evenly coated.
  6. Place the wings on a wire rack over a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 40–50 minutes, turning halfway through.
  8. Cook until the wings are golden, crispy, and cooked through.

For Buffalo Sauce:

  1. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over a low heat.
  2. Add the hot sauce, white vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Stir until smooth and fully combined.
  4. Remove from the heat.
  5. Place the cooked wings into a large bowl.
  6. Pour the Buffalo sauce over the wings.
  7. Toss until all the wings are evenly coated.
