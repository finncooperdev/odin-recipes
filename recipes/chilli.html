The BEST Chilli Con Carne

Description

This recipe has been tested over many years to create a rich, spicy, and delicious chilli con carne that you will make over and over again!

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 1 tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion and pepper for 3–4 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
  4. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, and tomato purée.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes, kidney beans, and crumbled beef stock cube. Stir well.
  6. Simmer on a low heat for around 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
