Easy Weeknight Spaghetti

Description

This will be your favourite weeknight friendly spaghetti recipe that you can enjoy alone or with the family.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs or Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti according to the packet instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
  5. Stir in the tomato purée, mixed herbs, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  6. Add the chopped tomatoes and crumbled beef stock cube. Stir well.
  7. Leave the sauce to simmer for around 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Drain the spaghetti and serve with the Bolognese sauce on top.
  9. Finish with grated Parmesan cheese if desired.
